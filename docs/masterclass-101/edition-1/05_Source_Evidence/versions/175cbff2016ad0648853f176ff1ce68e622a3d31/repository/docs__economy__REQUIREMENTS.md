## Exhaustive checks and sampled evidence

For a fully enumerated finite assignment domain under `max_plans`, the conventional procedure considers every combination of registered fixed lots and eligible provider windows. The Harness's pruning uses nonnegative lower bounds and capacity constraints; parity is checked on registered and targeted variations. Both refuse to call a domain exhaustive when its configured search bound is exceeded. This scope excludes arbitrary contract transformations, unrestricted negotiations and unknown capabilities.

Every accepted ordinal, declared label field, imported unit and recorded ledger transition is inspected in a run; that is exhaustive over the retained finite data. Crash tests enumerate named transition hooks, and contention tests use explicit barriers and seeds. They sample a failure model rather than all operating-system schedules, hardware failures or adversaries. Sequential replacement, late observations and coherent evidence mutations receive targeted controls; successful controls do not remove host/controller/service trust.

## Unestablished claims

No test establishes real customer authority, genuine voluntary demand, external certification, actual payments, rendered or printed output, complete labor cost, general AI performance, commercial scalability or general autonomy. An authorized pilot needs an authentic versioned digital obligation and independently agreed receiving standard, real consent/disclosure terms, measured resources and costs, bounded failure/settlement policy and independently retained evidence custody. Those are additional evidence requirements, not claims made by this local release candidate.
