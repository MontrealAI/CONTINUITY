## The promise and its source records

**K0 · Frozen promise P1**
Printer A owes 10,000 packaging labels to its customer. They must be received and accepted by Thursday 12 November 2026, 16:00, America/Toronto, at the fictional Montréal warehouse, dock 3, site M1. Morgan is the customer’s named acceptance lead. No short shipment or quality substitution is authorized.

| Acceptance item | Printed teaching specification |
| --- | --- |
| Artwork / material | Approved artwork v3; matte white self-adhesive stock. |
| Dimensions / quality | 50 × 30 mm, tolerance ±0.5 mm; approved readable code; damaged labels do not count. |
| Receipt / decision | Morgan checks count, specification, quality, place and receipt/acceptance times. |
| Disruption | Printer A’s order system and production are unavailable; its normal artwork login is also unavailable. |

**K1 · Reconstruct the obligation**
The restored checkpoint covers Tuesday 10 November, 12:00 and shows 8,000 labels. Authorized amendment A-02, recorded Tuesday at 14:00, adds 2,000. Custodian log C-01 covers through the frozen cutoff: Wednesday 11 November, 12:00, America/Toronto. It lists no cancellation or further amendment. The supplied log is separately accessible from the affected system.

Treat these printed records as exercise assumptions. A real use would need authenticated origin, authority and coverage through the cutoff. A checksum alone cannot establish completeness. The original customer invoice is outside the continuity-service ledger. [C1, pp. 9–11]

## Two records, two separate histories

Use K0–K4 from this workbook. Cover K5–K6 until requested. Agree on a route, its terms and funding first; then release K5. Your outputs are the decision log and Capstone Passport.

**K5 · R1 receipt and failure invoice**
At 15:40 on 12 November, mock receipt R1 records 9,800 conforming v3 labels at M1. Morgan acknowledges those units but does not give P1 full acceptance. The 4,700 preacceptance costs were paid; the 3,500 advance was applied to documented eligible nonrecoverable costs. The simulated inspector invoices 300, due now even after failure: this explicit failure trigger supplements K4. No 2,100 balance payment or repair agreement is supplied. Any unused advance would remain refundable.

**K6 · Optional separate positive control**
R2 is a separate positive-control tabletop branch from a fresh assumed starting state, with its own simulated approval and the same K0–K4 resources. Its new mock receipt records all 10,000 conforming labels received and accepted by Morgan at M1 at 15:40. A separate learner checks the records. Simulated settlement records receipt of the 2,100 balance and payment of the 300 inspection charge.

Do not replace R1 or use R2’s modeled money to settle it. Preserve R1 costs, debts and unresolved repair duties. Neither branch establishes actual manufacturing, actual payment or a real market. A physical rerun would require new authorization, resources and treatment of prior obligations.
