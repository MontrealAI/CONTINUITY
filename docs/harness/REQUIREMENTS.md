## Finite, selected and held-out coverage

`FiniteLedgerTests.test_exhaustive_three_step_ledger_traces` enumerates exactly **432 traces**: two capacities (1 and 2) × six actions to the third power. There are two one-unit obligations; actions are reserve A, reserve B, commit A, fulfill A, release A and expire. Traces have length three at ticks 0, 1 and 2, with expiry at tick 2. This exhausts that finite action domain only. It does not exhaust longer traces, arbitrary quantities, commit/fulfill B, every withdrawal history or all operating-system schedules.

Crashes, reversed barrier orders, partial file/pipe writes, permission changes, input mutations, deadline cases and the three verifier mutations are selected adversarial checks. They complement the finite model. Tests that deliberately give a trusted fixture resealing power challenge semantics without granting that power to ordinary candidates.

The registered comparison contains **24 conventional/Harness execution cells** (four fixtures × three controls × two methods) and **12 AI entries marked NOT RUN** without an authorized local model. These fixtures are held out from the base demonstration, not secret or statistically representative. Run identities differ between invocations; seeds and business conditions stay fixed. Unsuccessful attempts remain separately recorded rather than silently pooled as independent trials.

## Measurements and real-world boundary

Execution wall time, child CPU, controller CPU when recorded, storage, broker requests, retries and restarts have stated measurement boundaries. Verification/reporting CPU, peak memory, preparation/integration labor, monetary rates and complete business costs stay unknown where unrecorded. A configured currency or zero fixture budget cannot become a measured cost. A functional tie is valid; missing complete costs cannot establish that one method is cheaper.

The milestone requires feasible recovery and alternate execution, an exact constrained result, observed required faults and no critical safety failure in the tested model. Release, branch push, PR, merge, deployment and accepted customer rehearsal are distinct states.

Before an authorized real-world rehearsal, the smallest additional evidence includes genuine order authority/custody, current qualified provider capacity, disclosure/activation permissions, agreed acceptance and clocks, an isolated operational plan and complete commercial obligations. See [REAL_PILOT.md](../continuity-omega/REAL_PILOT.md). Synthetic software tests do not supply those facts.
